Adds RetroAchievements to DK64 Rekongpiled, with achievement popups, progress tracking, and a badge browser you can open with **F8**.

What's new:

- A badge grid you can browse with a mouse, keyboard, or controller.
- An **Account** panel for signing in and out, plus a **Save** checkbox in the Windows sign-in window.
- New trophy artwork and a cleaner interface.
- Fixed ROM detection for standard Windows installs.

Download the ZIP for your platform below, or grab **dk64-ra.zip** for both Windows and Linux. [Installation instructions](https://github.com/Nikamimi/DK64Rekong-RetroAchievements#install). Linux support is experimental.

Recommended to play with Vanilla DK Rekongpiled. Compatibility with other mods is not guaranteed, but feel free to try.
